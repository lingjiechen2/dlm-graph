==============================================================================
  Link-prediction SFT prompts (task = lp)
==============================================================================

Real samples produced by dllm.data.graph.build_edge_sample for both
supported datasets. Each LP sample asks the model to judge whether
two papers cite each other given each paper's title+abstract and a
small neighbourhood for each.

Sequence layout (all body tokens, no chat template):

  Paper A: <u.title>. <u.abstract>
  Neighbor A1: <u_nb_1.title>. <u_nb_1.abstract>
  ... up to max_neighbors_per_hop × max_hops kept
  Paper B: <v.title>. <v.abstract>
  Neighbor B1: ...
  Do Paper A and Paper B cite each other? Answer: <yes|no>

Labels are -100 everywhere except the final answer token. node_spans
+ node_roles enumerate each region (0=TARGET, 1=NBR_U, 2=NBR_V,
3=QUESTION).

Leak prevention:
  - cora_lp / arxiv_lp build `adj_train` with all val+test positives
    removed before neighbour sampling.
  - _sample_lp_neighbors additionally filters v from u's pool and
    vice versa; shared neighbours appear under one side only.
